================ N-s2 ANTICIPATION: APA-STYLE RESULTS ================

Analyses restricted to the noun's second syllable (N-s2), 1800 tokens
(900 present/paroxytone, 900 past/oxytone). Duration, intensity, and mean
F0 were each modelled with linear mixed-effects models (condition fixed;
by-participant and by-item random effects); F0 contour shape was tested with
an ordered-factor GAMM difference smooth over within-syllable time.

Duration. N-s2 was reliably shorter in the past/oxytone condition,
  b = -9.51 ms, SE = 1.35, t = -7.02, p < .001.
Intensity. No condition difference, b = +0.02 dB, p = .898.
F0 height. No difference in mean F0, b = +0.21 st, p = .378.
F0 shape. The within-syllable F0 trajectory differed by condition,
  F(1.00) = 6.41, p = .011 (edf ~ 1, i.e. a near-linear tilt difference).

With 4 tests, a Bonferroni threshold of alpha = 0.0125 was applied:
the duration effect survives comfortably; the F0-shape effect is marginal
(p just above the corrected threshold) and is reported as suggestive. No
anticipatory cue was found in intensity or in mean F0.
